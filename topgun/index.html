Welcome to Top Gun

Top Gun is a 1986 American action drama film directed by Tony Scott. The movie stars Tom Cruise as Lieutenant Pete "Maverick" Mitchell, a talented fighter pilot who is selected to attend the United States Navy's elite fighter weapons school.

The movie combines aviation, friendship, competition, romance, and personal challenges. It became one of the most recognizable aviation movies in American popular culture.

Movie Information

  • Release Year: 1986
  • Director: Tony Scott
  • Starring: Tom Cruise
  • Genre: Action / Drama
  • Setting: United States Navy aviation
