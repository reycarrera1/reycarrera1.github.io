Top Gun's Legacy

Top Gun became one of the most recognizable aviation movies of the 1980s. The film helped make fighter aircraft, military aviation, and naval aviation more familiar to a wide audience.

The movie is also remembered for its soundtrack, flying scenes, characters, and memorable dialogue.

Important Elements of the Movie
