The F-14 Tomcat

The Grumman F-14 Tomcat is one of the most recognizable aircraft associated with the original Top Gun movie. It was a twin-engine, two-seat fighter aircraft operated by the United States Navy.

The aircraft was designed for carrier-based operations and became famous for its variable-sweep wings.

Interesting Aircraft Facts

  • The F-14 was designed for the U.S. Navy.
  • The F-14 has variable-sweep wings.
  • The aircraft has two engines.
  • The F-14 has a two-person crew.
  • The aircraft became strongly associated with Top Gun.
