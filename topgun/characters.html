Character Descriptions

Maverick
A talented and ambitious fighter pilot who wants to prove himself at the Top Gun school.
Iceman
One of the top students at the school and one of Maverick's main competitors.
Goose
Maverick's radar intercept officer and close friend.
Charlie
A civilian instructor who teaches at the Top Gun school and develops a relationship with Maverick.
Viper
An experienced instructor and senior Navy officer who helps train the pilots.
